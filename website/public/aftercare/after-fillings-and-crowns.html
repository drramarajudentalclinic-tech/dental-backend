After fillings, crowns and bridges

Care after a tooth-coloured filling, a temporary crown, or a new crown or bridge.

These are general instructions. Always follow the specific advice given to you at your visit, and keep this page open on your phone for the first few days.

Call +91 99088 94449WhatsApp the clinic

Do

  • Wait until the numbness wears off before eating.
  • With a temporary crown, chew on the other side and slide floss out sideways instead of pulling it up.
  • Brush and floss around the new filling or crown; clean under a bridge with a floss threader or interdental brush.
  • Keep your review appointment so the bite and the fit can be checked.

Avoid

  • Avoid sticky or very hard food on a temporary crown.
  • Do not bite on pens, nails, ice or bottle caps; this can chip fillings and crowns.

What is normal

  • Mild sensitivity to cold for a few days.
  • The new filling or crown may feel slightly different to your tongue for a day or two.

Call the clinic if

  • The bite feels high or the tooth hurts when you bite.
  • A temporary crown comes off: keep it and bring it to the clinic.
  • Sharp pain or swelling.
  • A crown or bridge feels loose.
Emergency: If you have difficulty breathing or swallowing, swelling spreading to the eye or neck, or very heavy bleeding, go to the nearest hospital emergency department straight away.
