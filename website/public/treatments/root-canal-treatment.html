How a root canal works

  1. Infected nerveDecay reaches the nerve inside the tooth; it becomes inflamed and an infection can form at the root tip.
  2. Cleaning the canalsThe tooth is numbed, opened from the top, and the canals are cleaned and shaped with fine files.
  3. Filling and sealingThe cleaned canals are filled with gutta-percha and a sealer so germs cannot get back in.
  4. Crown protects the toothA filling or crown covers the tooth so it can be used for chewing again.

Root canal treatment: how it is done at our clinic

  1. Digital X-ray (RVG) to see the root shape, the number of canals and the infection.
  2. Local anaesthesia given slowly with the anaesthesia booster; the tooth is isolated to keep it clean and dry.
  3. The canals are opened and their exact length is measured with the apex locator, confirmed on an X-ray.
  4. A glide path with fine hand files, then cleaning and shaping with nickel-titanium rotary or reciprocating files on the endo motor (the file system chosen for your canals), with disinfecting rinses between files.
  5. The disinfecting solution is activated inside each canal with the endo activator, to reach side branches the files cannot touch.
  6. A gutta-percha master point matched to the canal is fitted and checked; it is coated with a bioactive (bioceramic) root canal sealer.
  7. Three-dimensional sealing: the obturation pen packs warm gutta-percha at the root tip, and the Cicada DT Fill fills the rest of the canal.
  8. A final X-ray to check the seal, and a filling to close the tooth.
  9. Where advised, a crown: a digital scan with the Runyes scanner; before cementing, the inside of the crown is cleaned with the micro-blaster and, after try-in, with Ivoclean.

Equipment used

  • Digital X-ray (RVG) — Shows the root shape and infection; checks the length and the final seal
  • Anaesthesia booster — Gives the numbing injection slowly and steadily
  • Apex locator — Measures the exact length of each canal
  • Endo motor — Turns the rotary files that clean and shape the canals
  • Endo activator — Activates the disinfecting solution inside the canals
  • Gutta-percha points — The master point matched to the canal size and taper
  • BioActive RCS — Bioceramic sealer that seals the gaps and supports healing
  • Obturation pen — Packs warm gutta-percha at the root tip
  • Cicada DT Fill — Fills the rest of the canal with warm gutta-percha
  • Runyes scanner — Digital scan for the crown, where a crown is advised
  • Micro-blaster — Cleans the inside of the crown before cementing
  • Ivoclean — Cleans the crown's bonding surface after try-in

Common questions

Is root canal treatment painful?

The tooth is numbed with local anaesthesia before treatment, so you should feel pressure but not pain. Some soreness for a day or two afterwards is common, and the doctor prescribes medicine for it.

Clean and safe

Used instruments are soaked in disinfectant and cleaned. They are packed and sealed in sterilisation pouches with a colour-change indicator. The pouches go through a steam autoclave cycle; the indicator changes colour to show the cycle was complete. Pouches are opened in front of you, at the chair, for your treatment.
