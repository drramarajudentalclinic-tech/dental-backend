How a dental implant is placed

  1. Missing toothA gap is left after a tooth is lost or removed. The bone and gum have healed.
  2. Surgical guide placedA 3-D printed guide, made from your scan, fits over the teeth. A small opening is made in the gum.
  3. Implant site preparedThrough the guide, the site is shaped in the bone step by step, cooled with water.
  4. Implant placedThe titanium implant is turned gently into the bone at the planned depth and angle.
  5. Bone heals around the implantA healing cap is placed. The bone grows onto the implant surface; the time differs for each patient.
  6. Connector fittedAfter healing, a connector (abutment) is screwed onto the implant.
  7. Crown fixedA tooth-coloured crown, made from a digital scan, is fixed on the connector.

Dental implant with a surgical guide: how it is done at our clinic

  1. Examination, medical history, a CBCT scan at an imaging centre and a digital scan of your teeth with the intra-oral scanner.
  2. Digital planning of implant size, position and angle, and a 3-D printed surgical guide.
  3. The implant system is chosen for your bone and budget (Straumann, Nobel Biocare, Osstem, DIO, Neo Biotech, Noris, Adin or Alpha-Bio).
  4. Local anaesthesia with the anaesthesia booster, and the implant placed through the guide.
  5. Only where bone is thin or healing needs support: a graft from your own extracted tooth (Tooth Titan) and PRF from your own blood (PRFuge).
  6. Healing period of about 3 months while the implant joins the bone (longer if grafted).
  7. A digital scan of the implant, then the crown is fitted: a screw-retained crown is screwed onto the implant, while a cemented crown is first cleaned inside with the micro-blaster. The bite is checked, with the Teeth Tracer where needed.
  8. Review visits and cleaning to keep the implant healthy.

Equipment used

  • Intra-oral scanner — Digital scan for planning, and later for the crown
  • Surgical guide — Places the implant in the planned position, depth and angle
  • Implant systems — The implant chosen for your bone and budget
  • Anaesthesia booster — Numbing before the implant is placed
  • PRFuge — PRF from your own blood to support healing
  • PRF & GRF box — Shapes the PRF into membranes and plugs
  • Tooth Titan — Graft from your own extracted tooth where bone is thin
  • Micro-blaster — Cleans the inside of a cemented crown (not needed for screw-retained crowns)
  • Teeth Tracer — Fine-tunes the bite on the implant crown

Common questions

How long does a dental implant take?

The implant is placed in one sitting under local anaesthesia. It then needs about 3 months to join the bone before the crown is fitted. If a bone graft is needed, healing can take longer; the doctor explains the timeline for your case.

Clean and safe

Used instruments are soaked in disinfectant and cleaned. They are packed and sealed in sterilisation pouches with a colour-change indicator. The pouches go through a steam autoclave cycle; the indicator changes colour to show the cycle was complete. Pouches are opened in front of you, at the chair, for your treatment.
